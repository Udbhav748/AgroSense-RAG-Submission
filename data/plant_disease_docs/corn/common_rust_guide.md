# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Corn Common Rust

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Etiology
- **Crop**: Field Corn, Sweet Corn, and Seed Corn (*Zea mays*)
- **Disease**: Common Rust
- **Causal Pathogen**: *Puccinia sorghi* Schwein.
- **Pathogen Classification**: Basidiomycete / Pucciniales (Obligate biotrophic rust fungus)
- **Alternate Host**: *Oxalis* species (woodsorrel), though urediniospores blown northward from southern subtropical regions initiate the vast majority of commercial field infections.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Pustules (Uredinia)**: Small, circular to elongate, cinnamon-brown to brick-red powdery pustules appear scattered randomly across **both the upper and lower leaf surfaces**.
- **Epidermal Rupture**: As pustules mature, the corn leaf epidermis ruptures cleanly, exposing powdery red-brown urediniospores that easily rub off onto fingers or clothing.
- **Distinction from Southern Corn Rust**:
  - *Common Rust (*Puccinia sorghi*)*: Pustules form on **both leaf surfaces**, are brownish-red, elongated/oval, and thrive in cooler temps (16°C to 24°C).
  - *Southern Rust (*Puccinia polysora*)*: Pustules form predominantly on the **upper leaf surface**, are bright golden-orange, circular, dense, and thrive in hot temps (28°C to 34°C).
- **Telia Formation**: Late in the season, pustules turn brownish-black as dark two-celled teliospores replace urediniospores.

---

## 3. Favorable Environmental Conditions & Spore Dispersal
- **Temperature Optimum**: 16°C to 24°C (60°F to 75°F). Pathogen development is markedly slowed or arrested when temperatures exceed 30°C (86°F).
- **Moisture Requirements**: Free water on leaves (heavy dew or rainfall) for 4 to 6 hours combined with relative humidity > 95%.
- **Long-Distance Aerial Transport**: Millions of urediniospores are carried northward annually from the Gulf Coast and Mexico along low-level jet streams ("Puccinia pathway").

---

## 4. Chemical Fungicide Spray Thresholds & Options

In field corn, treatment is economically justified if rust pustules appear on upper leaves before silking on susceptible hybrids. In fresh-market sweet corn, early fungicide protection is critical to prevent unmarketable husk leaf spotting.

## 5. Genetic Resistance & Integrated Management
- **Qualitative (Rp) Genes**: Hybrids carrying single-gene resistance (*Rp1-D*, *Rp1-E*, *Rp1-J*) confer complete immunity to specific rust races.
- **Quantitative / Partial Resistance**: Hybrids with general field tolerance show fewer, smaller pustules with delayed sporulation, preventing economic yield loss.
- **Planting Dates**: Early-planted corn often tassels and fills grain before spore clouds arrive from the south.
