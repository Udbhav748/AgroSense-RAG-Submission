# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Northern Corn Leaf Blight (NCLB)

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Etiology
- **Crop**: Field Corn, Sweet Corn, and Popcorn (*Zea mays*)
- **Disease**: Northern Corn Leaf Blight (NCLB)
- **Causal Pathogen**: *Exserohilum turcicum* (teleomorph *Setosphaeria turcica*)
- **Pathogen Classification**: Ascomycete / Pleosporaceae
- **Significance**: Causes massive photosynthetic leaf destruction and premature stalk senescence when infections occur around tasseling (VT) to silking (R1).

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Cigar-Shaped Lesions (Diagnostic)**: Long, elliptical, spindle- or "cigar-shaped" grayish-green to tan lesions (2.5 to 15 cm long by 1 to 3 cm wide).
- **Smooth Margins**: Unlike Gray Leaf Spot (which is vein-bound and rectangular), NCLB lesions cross veins smoothly, forming large elliptical blighted patches.
- **Dark Sporulation**: In damp weather, abundant dark olive-black spores form across the lesion surface, giving it a dirty, dusty appearance.
- **Whole-Plant Blight**: Leaves become completely gray-brown and necrotic, giving entire fields the appearance of being scorched by frost.

---

## 3. Integrated Disease Management & Chemical Rotation

| Strategy | Control Measure | Application / Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genetic Resistance** | Single-gene (*Ht1*, *Ht2*, *Ht3*, *Htn1*) | Plant hybrids with specific *Ht* resistance genes and strong quantitative background tolerance. |
| **Crop Rotation & Tillage** | 1 to 2-year rotation out of corn | Rotate to soybeans or small grains; deep-till heavy corn residue in high-risk zones. |
| **Fungicide Spray (VT/R1)** | Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole (Quilt Xcel) | 0.8 to 1.0 L/ha applied at VT/R1 when lesions appear on the third leaf below the ear. |
| **Fungicide Spray (VT/R1)** | Pyraclostrobin + Metconazole (Headline AMP) | 0.7 to 1.0 L/ha for extended 21-day residual foliar protection. |
