# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Grape Esca (Black Measles)

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Grapevine Trunk Disease Complex
- **Crop**: Grape (*Vitis vinifera*)
- **Disease**: Esca / Black Measles / Petri Disease
- **Causal Pathogen Complex**: *Phaeomoniella chlamydospora*, *Phaeoacremonium minimum*, and wood-rotting basidiomycetes (*Fomitiporia mediterranea*).
- **Classification**: Complex fungal trunk disease that invades pruning wounds and colonizes vascular xylem tissue.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Tiger-Striped Foliage (Diagnostic)**: Spectacular interveinal chlorosis and necrosis. Leaf tissue between main veins turns yellow, dries out, and turns reddish-brown to dark brown, while narrow green bands remain along the primary veins ("tiger-stripe" pattern).
- **Black Measles on Berries**: Clusters develop small, dark purplish-brown to black flecks or speckles resembling measles. Severely affected fruit cracks, shrivels, and becomes unmarketable.
- **Apoplexy (Sudden Vine Collapse)**: During mid-summer heat spells with high transpiration demand, entire vines or cordons wilt, dry up, and die within a few days due to vascular xylem occlusion.
- **Internal Trunk Symptoms**: Cross-section of infected trunk reveals dark brown spots in xylem vessels (gumming) and soft, spongy yellow/white central heartwood decay ("amadou").

---

## 3. Prevention & Pruning Wound Management
- **Pruning Wound Pastes**: Apply protective fungicides (Thiophanate-methyl / Topsin-M paste) or bio-protectants containing *Trichoderma atroviride* (Esquive WP) within 24 hours of making pruning cuts.
- **Delayed / Double Pruning**: Delay final pruning to late winter when wound susceptibility declines and ambient spore release is lower.
- **Vine Renewal (Trunk Surgery)**: In mature infected vines, perform remedial trunk renewal by retraining a healthy basal sucker from below the cankered wood.
