# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Peach Bacterial Spot

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Etiology
- **Crop**: Peach & Nectarine (*Prunus persica*)
- **Disease**: Bacterial Spot / Shot Hole / Bacteriosis
- **Causal Pathogen**: *Xanthomonas arboricola* pv. *pruni* (Smith) Vauterin et al.
- **Classification**: Gram-negative Xanthomonadaceae bacterium
- **Damage Profile**: Serious disease in sandy soils and windy orchard sites, causing severe foliar shot-holing, defoliation, twig dieback, and pitted, unmarketable fruit.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Foliar Shot-Hole Lesions**: Small (1 to 3 mm), angular, purple to dark brown water-soaked spots appear on the leaf blade, concentrated along the midrib and leaf tip. As spot centers dry, they crack and drop out cleanly, leaving distinct "shot-holes" with a ragged purplish margin. Leaves turn yellow and drop prematurely.
- **Spring Twig Cankers**: Develop on previous season's green twigs as water-soaked, dark blisters that erupt in spring, exuding bacterial ooze during wet weather.
- **Fruit Pitting & Gumming**: Small olive-brown water-soaked spots on young green fruit. Spots enlarge, turn dark brown to black, and crack deeply into the fruit flesh. Clear amber gum frequently exudes from deep fruit cracks.

---

## 3. Chemical Spray & Resistance Mitigation Protocol

| Growth Stage | Chemical / Bactericide | Active Ingredient & Rate | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dormant / Late Bud Swell** | Copper Hydroxide 53.8% WG | 3.0 to 4.0 g/L (Kocide 3000) | High rate to sanitize overwintering twig cankers. |
| **Shuck Split to Petal Fall** | Low-Rate Fixed Copper / Dodine | 0.5 g/L Copper Hydroxide or Dodine 65WP (1.0 g/L) | Reduced copper rate to avoid severe fruit russeting. |
| **Cover Sprays (Fruit Sizing)** | Oxytetracycline (Mycoshield) | 1.0 to 1.5 g/L (where registered) | Rotated with low-dose copper to suppress fruit infection. |
| **Foliar Bio-pesticide** | *Bacillus subtilis* strain QST 713 | 2.5 to 4.0 g/L (Serenade ASO) | Preventative biological protection. |
