# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV)

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Vector Dynamics
- **Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Disease**: Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV)
- **Pathogen**: Begomovirus (Family *Geminiviridae*) - single-stranded circular DNA virus.
- **Insect Vector**: Silverleaf Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci* Gennadius / MEAM1 and MED cryptic species complexes). The virus is acquired within 15 to 30 minutes of phloem feeding and transmitted persistently and circulative for the lifespan of the adult whitefly.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Stunting**: Severe plant stunting with shortened internodes, creating a bushy, erect, bonsai-like growth habit.
- **Foliar Distortion**: New leaflets are markedly reduced in size, with upward cupping / curling of leaf margins ("spooning"). Interveinal chlorosis is pronounced with vibrant yellowing along leaf edges.
- **Flower Abscission**: Flowers abort and drop before pollination. If plants are infected at seedling or early vegetative stages, fruit set is reduced to near zero.
- **Fruit Symptoms**: Existing fruit remains small, hard, and fails to ripen evenly.

---

## 3. Integrated Vector & Virus Management Protocol

Because there is **no chemical cure** for an established viral infection, all management focuses on genetic host resistance, vector suppression, and physical exclusion.

### Genetic Host Resistance (Primary Line of Defense)
- Plant commercial hybrid cultivars with verified *Ty-1*, *Ty-2*, *Ty-3*, or *Ty-5* resistance alleles (e.g., 'Tygress', 'Charger', 'Grand Marshall', 'Tribute', 'Red Deuce', 'Saybrook').

### Vector Suppression & Exclusion

| Management Component | Active Ingredient / Measure | Rate / Timing | Action Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Barrier** | 50-mesh anti-insect netting | Full enclosure in nurseries | Physically excludes whitefly adults from entering transplant houses. |
| **Reflective Mulch** | Metalized UV-reflective silver plastic | At bed shaping | Repels migrating adult whiteflies during early vegetative growth. |
| **Systemic Insecticide** | Thiamethoxam 25% WG / Imidacloprid | 0.3 g/L or drench at transplant | Neonicotinoid (IRAC 4A) systemic protection for 30 to 45 days. |
| **Lipid Synthesis Inhibitor** | Spirotetramat 150 OD (Movento) | 0.5 ml/L foliar spray | IRAC 23; two-way systemic activity controlling immature whitefly nymphs. |
| **Biological Insecticide** | *Beauveria bassiana* strain GHA (BotaniGard) | 2.5 ml/L foliar spray | Entomopathogenic fungal bio-control of whiteflies. |
| **Sanitation Rogueing** | Bag & Remove Protocol | Immediate | Bag symptomatic plants in clear plastic before uprooting to trap resident whiteflies. |
