# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Citrus Greening (Huanglongbing - HLB)

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Vector Dynamics
- **Crop**: Citrus species (Sweet Orange, Grapefruit, Mandarin, Lemon, Lime)
- **Disease**: Citrus Greening / Huanglongbing (HLB) / Yellow Dragon Disease
- **Causal Pathogen**: *Candidatus* Liberibacter asiaticus (CLas) - fastidious, phloem-limited, uncultured Gram-negative bacterium.
- **Insect Vector**: Asian Citrus Psyllid (*Diaphorina citri* Kuwayama). Psyllids acquire CLas during nymphal or adult feeding on phloem sap and transmit the bacterium persistently across citrus groves.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Blotchy Mottle (Diagnostic Foliar Sign)**: Asymmetrical, patchy yellowing and mottling across the leaf blade. Unlike nutritional deficiencies (which are symmetrical along veins), HLB blotchy mottle is **completely asymmetrical across the midrib**.
- **Yellow Shoot ("Huanglongbing")**: A single bright yellow shoot stands out prominently against the dark green canopy of an infected tree.
- **Vein Corking**: Older leaves develop raised, swollen, corky midribs and lateral veins that crack.
- **Lopsided, Inverted Ripening Fruit**: Fruit remains small, hard, and distinctly asymmetrical or lopsided. Fruit ripens in reverse (color breaks from the stem-end while the stylar bottom remains green, hence "citrus greening"). The juice is bitter, acidic, and low in sugars; internal seeds are aborted and dark brown.
- **Dieback**: Rapid canopy thinning, root starvation, twig dieback, and tree mortality within 3 to 5 years.

---

## 3. Comprehensive HLB Control & Psyllid Vector Program

Because CLas resides inside the vascular phloem, foliar bactericides cannot eradicate it. Effective management requires a rigorous 3-pronged approach:

### 1. Vector (Asian Citrus Psyllid) Chemical Suppression
- **Systemic Soil Drench**: Imidacloprid 2F or Thiamethoxam 25WG applied around root zone of young non-bearing trees for 60 to 90-day systemic protection.
- **Foliar Knockdown Sprays**: Rotate IRAC modes of action across flush cycles:
  - Dimethoate 4EC / Chlorpyrifos (IRAC 1B)
  - Zeta-cypermethrin / Bifenthrin (IRAC 3A)
  - Spirotetramat (Movento 150OD, IRAC 23) to suppress psyllid nymphs.
  - Horticultural mineral oil (1% to 2%) as physical oviposition deterrent.

### 2. Enhanced Foliar Nutrition & Root Care
- Apply frequent foliar micronutrient sprays (Zinc, Manganese, Boron, Iron, and soluble Potassium Phosphite) to sustain phloem function and canopy vigor in infected bearing trees.

### 3. Biological Control & Sanitation
- Release parasitoid wasps (*Tamarixia radiata*) in citrus groves to parasitoidize psyllid nymphs.
- Enclose new citrus nursery stock inside insect-proof screens (CUPS - Citrus Under Protective Screens).
