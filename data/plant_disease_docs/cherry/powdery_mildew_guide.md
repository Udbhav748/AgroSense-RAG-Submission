# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Cherry Powdery Mildew

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Etiology
- **Crop**: Sweet Cherry (*Prunus avium*) & Sour Cherry (*Prunus cerasus*)
- **Disease**: Powdery Mildew
- **Causal Pathogen**: *Podosphaera clandestina* (Wallr.: Fr.) Lev.
- **Classification**: Ascomycete / Erysiphaceae
- **Economic Loss**: Causes foliar distortion and white powdery fungal patches directly on sweet cherry fruit skin, rendering fruit unmarketable for fresh shipping.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Foliar Symptoms**: Light green to yellowish circular patches on young terminal leaves. A white powdery fungal growth covers the lower leaf surface, followed by upward leaf curling, blistering, and crinkling of leaf margins.
- **Fruit Symptoms**: Developing green fruit exhibits circular patches of white powdery fungal hyphae. Infected fruit skin becomes sunken, deformed, and leathery, preventing normal ripening.
- **Chasmothecia (Overwintering Sign)**: Late in summer, tiny yellow-to-black spherical fruiting structures (chasmothecia) form embedded within the white mycelial mats on leaves and twigs.
