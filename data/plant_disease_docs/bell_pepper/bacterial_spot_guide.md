# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Bell Pepper Bacterial Spot

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Etiology
- **Crop**: Bell Pepper, Chili Pepper (*Capsicum annuum*, *Capsicum frutescens*)
- **Disease**: Bacterial Spot
- **Causal Pathogens**: *Xanthomonas euvesicatoria*, *Xanthomonas vesicatoria*, *Xanthomonas perforans*, and *Xanthomonas gardneri*
- **Pathogen Type**: Gram-negative proteobacteria
- **Economic Loss**: Causes extensive foliar drop, flower bud abortion, and severe blistered fruit lesions leading to secondary rots.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Foliar Spots**: Small, circular to angular, water-soaked, dark green spots appear on leaves. Spot centers dry into tan-brown lesions surrounded by a yellow halo. Spots often become prominent on the lower leaf surface.
- **Severe Defoliation**: Pepper leaves are exceptionally sensitive; multiple lesions cause entire leaves to drop quickly while still partially green, completely defoliating plants and leaving pepper pods exposed to severe sunscald.
- **Fruit Blisters & Cankers**: Young pepper fruit develop small, slightly raised, blister-like pale green spots that turn brown, rough, scabby, and crateriform (up to 5 mm). Fruit skin cracks, allowing entry of soft-rot bacteria (*Pectobacterium*).

---

## 3. Management Protocols & Chemical Bactericides

| Method | Specification | Rate / Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genetic Resistance** | Races 0–10 resistance (*Bs1*, *Bs2*, *Bs3*, *Bs4*) | Plant multi-race resistant hybrids (e.g., 'Aristotle X3R', 'Playmaker', 'Currier', 'Outsider', 'Touchdown'). |
| **Bactericide Tank Mix** | Copper Hydroxide 53.8% + Mancozeb 75% WP | 2.0 g/L + 2.0 g/L applied weekly before rain. |
| **Systemic Defense Activator** | Acibenzolar-S-methyl (Actigard 50WG) | 0.025 to 0.035 g/L foliar spray. |
| **Bio-bactericide** | *Bacillus amyloliquefaciens* strain D747 | 2.5 g/L biological preventative. |
