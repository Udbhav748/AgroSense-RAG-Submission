# Comprehensive Diagnostic & Management Guide: Potato Early Blight

## 1. Pathogen Overview & Etiology
- **Crop**: Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*)
- **Disease**: Early Blight & Alternaria Brown Spot
- **Causal Pathogens**: *Alternaria solani* Sorauer and *Alternaria alternata* (Fr.) Keissl.
- **Pathogen Classification**: Ascomycete / Pleosporaceae
- **Crop Stress Association**: Often attacks senescing foliage, plants stressed by low nitrogen, drought, heavy tuber bulking, or verticillium wilt.

---

## 2. Key Symptoms & Field Identification
- **Target-Board Spots**: Brown to black spots (3 to 12 mm) with concentric rings develop on older lower leaves. Spots are often bordered by a distinct yellow chlorotic halo.
- **Angular Leaf Distortion**: As spots expand, they are partially delimited by major leaf veins, creating angular margins before coalescing into large necrotic leaf areas.
- **Tuber Symptoms**: Dark, sunken, circular to irregular lesions on tuber skin with raised purplish-brown borders. The underlying dry flesh is corky, brown, and typically shallow (2 to 6 mm).

### Agronomic Nutrition
- Maintain adequate nitrogen and potassium levels throughout the mid-to-late tuber bulking phase to delay physiological foliar senescence and reduce early blight susceptibility.
